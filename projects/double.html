Double (December 1612), 2026
Painted birch, acrylic, mirror, light, electronics
11 × 24 × 7 in.
One-hour cycle, then off for ten minutes

A plaque outside 7 rue des Grands-Augustins in Paris records that Picasso lived and worked there, and that the building is the setting of Balzac’s The Unknown Masterpiece. Picasso moved in after reading it. Balzac’s painter, Frenhofer, believes he has made a masterpiece; when others finally see it, it is unreadable.

Double is two mirrored boxes. Inside each is a mirror engraved with the plaque’s text, lit intermittently from within, so the inscription and the viewer’s reflection appear on the same surface and then go.
