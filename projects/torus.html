Twisted Torus 2014

Twisted Torus, 2014
Series of sculptures
Mixed media

Twisted Torus is a series of sculptural studies of the torus, developed through digital modeling and physical fabrication. Twisting the surface and reshaping the profile produces forms in which a complex interior grows inside a distorted but continuous outer shell. Curved projection techniques then pierce the exterior to reveal part of that interior.

Process diagrams showing the development of the form and the articulation of the interior geometry.
