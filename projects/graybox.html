Gray Box 2025

Gray Box, 2025
Proposal
Painted wood, acrylic, light, electronics
About 20 × 17 × 14 in.

Andy Warhol’s Brillo Boxes (1964) are wooden boxes silkscreened to look like the cartons Brillo soap pads were shipped in. Gray Box is the same box at the same size, printed entirely in gray, so at first the design barely shows. A light inside slowly brings it up, but the red and blue never return. The clearer the image gets, the older it looks.
