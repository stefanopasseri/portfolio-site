Cast of Characters 2013

Cast of Characters, 2013
Extruded polystyrene, cut with a robotic hot wire
In collaboration with Anass Benachmi and David Eskenazi

Cast of Characters is a series of sculptures cut from large blocks of extruded polystyrene with a hot wire mounted on a robotic arm. The process gives sharp, intricate profiles, and the forms that come out of it are irregular and almost figurative. The foam is treated not as packing material but as something worth carving.

A robotic arm with a hot wire cutter mounted on it was used to cut the sculptures.
