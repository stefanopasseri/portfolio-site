Inflated, and deflated.

Ghost of Ghost, 2026
Muslin, ripstop nylon, steel conduit, casters, repurposed blower fan, vacuum hose, butterfly valves, motors, electronics, Velcro
84 × 48 × 16 in.
One-hour cycle, with a ten-minute pause

In 1964 Claes Oldenburg and Patty Mucha made Soft Light Switches — “Ghost” Version II: an ordinary wall switch, enlarged, sewn in canvas and stripped of its function. Ghost of Ghost is a full-size reconstruction that leaves the function out and puts a mechanism back in. A light switch is already a mechanism, so the sculpture is pulled two ways at once, toward the ordinary object and further away from it.

Over about an hour it fills, sags, partly collapses and flips its toggles. No light comes on.

Yoga mode.

Hyperventilating, dreaming, snoozing, deep breath (clockwise from top left).

From behind. Lean around it and the mechanism is in plain view.

Kit of parts. Pattern pieces and a half-scale single switch.

The back: bladder and frame, a fan from a yard inflatable, the microcontroller, a toggle’s motor, a valve prototype, a soft toggle. The soft parts hold onto the hard ones with snap fasteners made for baby clothes. Glue failed; duct tape held but looked terrible.
